# Analyze Market

Produce a sourced market and alternatives view without fake precision.

## Workflow

1. Read the current problem/user hypothesis first.
2. Run `/market-researcher` and `/competitor-analyst` in parallel when both are needed.
3. Include manual workarounds and "do nothing" as alternatives.
4. Cross-check important external claims where practical.
5. Update `market.md` and `competitors.md` with claim labels and sources.
6. Separate observed price anchors from willingness-to-pay assumptions.
7. Record meaningful counter-signals, not just opportunity-supporting facts.
8. Invoke `/verifier` for promotion-critical research.

## Output

End with:

- market implications;
- strongest existing alternative;
- possible differentiation spaces;
- unresolved evidence gaps;
- whether more research is likely to change the decision.
