# Market analysis checklist

A useful market artifact considers only relevant dimensions:

- category definition and boundaries;
- target segment behavior;
- current alternatives;
- demand/trend signals;
- pricing/value anchors;
- channel/platform dependencies;
- regulation or compliance;
- switching cost and lock-in;
- timing/tailwinds/headwinds;
- counter-signals.

Do not force a TAM/SAM/SOM section if credible inputs do not exist.
