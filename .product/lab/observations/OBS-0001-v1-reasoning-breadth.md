# OBS-0001 — v1 reasoning breadth

## Observation

During real v1.0.0 dogfooding, the user found the depth of analysis useful but wanted broader proposals and a wider field of view before convergence.

## Supported interpretation

The current orchestration appears stronger at deepening the initially framed problem than at deliberately surfacing materially different reframes, adjacent users/JTBD, alternative solution classes, non-product options, or contrarian directions.

## Improvement hypothesis

Before deep research, add an explicit perspective-expansion pass and compare it against the v1.0 Stable Kernel. This is a hypothesis, not yet an accepted Core change.

## First eval question

Does Adaptive Reasoning increase materially useful opportunity breadth without increasing unsupported claims, decision paralysis, or research cost beyond an acceptable level?
